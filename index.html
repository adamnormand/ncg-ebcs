Evidence in Action

We embrace the role of “scientist-practitioner” and use a combination of science and practice to enhance the well-being and performance of our clients and their employees. As scientists, our goal is to conduct research that is relevant, useful, and meaningful to our clients.

As practitioners, our goal is even simpler: to put this research into practice—to develop and deliver evidence-based programs and services that are founded in research, but tested in real workplaces. In short, we provide scientifically-evaluated programs and services that can be customized to:

  • maintain their scientific integrity, and
  • meet the unique needs and resources of the organization.

We know that choosing the right solution for your workplace is important. We work with you to understand your needs, goals and constraints and to identify practical and effective solutions.
